# S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 1 / Anomaly project

The S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 1 product line is a modular project designed around Anomaly.
It is separate from the S.T.A.L.K.E.R. 2 overhaul.

Active development, module source, integration data, tests, and acceptance
evidence are private. Internal development or successful technical tests do not
mean that a public modpack or installer is available.

There is currently no public download and no gameplay-acceptance claim. Any
future distribution of project-authored or third-party modules will require a
deliberate release decision and the applicable rights and permissions.
